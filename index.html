What To Do Before You Arrive

A few simple steps can protect your tooth and ease discomfort. Rinse your mouth with warm salt water, apply a cold compress to your cheek and avoid very hot or cold food. Take pain medicine only as directed on the package. If a tooth is knocked out, hold it by the crown, keep it moist in milk and bring it with you. If you have swelling that affects your eye, your breathing or your swallowing, go to the nearest hospital emergency room and call us afterward.

Prevention After the Emergency

Once the urgent problem is solved, we help you prevent the next one. We may suggest a night guard for grinding, a sealant for deep grooves, a crown for a weak tooth or a simple change in brushing technique. A few small habits, such as regular checkups, flossing daily and wearing a mouthguard in sports, greatly reduce the chance of another emergency.
